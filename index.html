Questions, answered

Where exactly is the apartment?

Rua de Santa Catarina 392, in the Santo Ildefonso district of central Porto. The street is pedestrian-only here, lined with shops and cafes, a short walk from Bolhao Market, the Capela das Almas tiled chapel and Bolhao metro station.

How many guests can stay?

Up to 2 guests. The apartment has one double bed in a dedicated sleeping area and one bathroom with a walk-in shower.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 16:00 and check-out is until 11:00. Details and any flexibility are confirmed with each booking on Airbnb.

Is the apartment good for remote work?

Yes. There is Wi-Fi and a dedicated workspace, and the living area gets daylight through three tall windows.

Is there a lift?

Yes. The apartment is on the second floor and the building has a lift. Ask through Airbnb about any entrance steps or specific access needs.

Is there a washing machine?

Yes, a washer and a dryer are inside the apartment, which makes longer stays easy.

Why choose this particular apartment?

Three tall French windows frame Rua de Santa Catarina from the second floor. Approximately 56 m², natural light and an open living space give two guests a welcoming base in central Porto.

How far is the apartment from Estádio do Dragão?

Bolhão metro station is about a one-minute walk from the apartment. From there, a direct metro ride to Estádio do Dragão takes about 7 to 8 minutes, with no changes. The stadium is home to FC Porto, has hosted Portugal's national team and is a 2030 FIFA World Cup venue. A convenient base for fans visiting Porto for a match.
